**AI-augmented software development** (using AI assistants and agents to explore code, draft changes, write tests, and review work) is now our default way of building software. Engineers remain accountable for every change, and AI output gets the same review and testing as anything else. We pair it with sandboxed workspaces, project instructions kept in the repository, and CLIs that agents can drive, and we invest in the review skills the shift demands.
