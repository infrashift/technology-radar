**HashiCorp Consul** provides service discovery, health checking, and a key/value store across our datacenter and cloud environments. Services register themselves and consumers find healthy instances through DNS or the API, so nobody hand-maintains host lists or load balancer pools. Consul pairs with Nomad for scheduling and Vault for secrets, and together they give us a cohesive, small-footprint platform.
