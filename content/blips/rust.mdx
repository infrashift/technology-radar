We're trialing **Rust** where its strengths matter most: performance-critical components, systems code, and anywhere memory safety bugs would be expensive. Its ownership model eliminates whole classes of bugs at compile time without a garbage collector. It's in trial rather than adopt because its learning curve and compile times make it a poor default for the services and tooling where Go serves us well.
