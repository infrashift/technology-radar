**WireGuard** is our VPN for connecting our private datacenter, Google Cloud, and engineers to the remote workspaces they work in. It's built into the Linux kernel, has a small codebase that's practical to audit, uses a fixed set of modern cryptographic primitives with nothing to negotiate, and is configured with little more than a key pair and a list of peers. It's faster and far simpler to operate than IPsec or OpenVPN.
