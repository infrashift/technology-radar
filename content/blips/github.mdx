We're placing **GitHub** on hold for new projects. Our source code, CI, and the provenance of our artifacts are core infrastructure, and we want them on infrastructure we control, alongside our private datacenter, rather than in a hosted service whose pricing, policies, and AI data practices we don't set. Existing repositories stay where they are while we assess GitLab.
