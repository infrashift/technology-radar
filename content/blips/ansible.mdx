**Ansible** is how we configure machines once they exist. It's agentless (it connects over SSH) and playbooks are readable YAML, so the same automation works for bare-metal servers in our datacenter and VMs in Google Cloud. Terraform provisions infrastructure; Ansible configures what runs on it.
