Every artifact we build ships with a **software bill of materials (SBOM)** in SPDX or CycloneDX format, generated in the build pipeline from the artifact itself rather than written by hand. SBOMs let us answer "are we affected?" within minutes when a new vulnerability is disclosed, and customers and regulators increasingly expect them. We attach them to the artifact as attestations, alongside its build provenance.
