**Ratatui** is the leading Rust library for building TUIs and the community-maintained successor to tui-rs. It renders immediate-mode widgets such as tables, charts, lists, and gauges with fine control over layout and good performance. We're trialing it for terminal tools written in Rust; Bubble Tea remains our default for TUIs in Go.
