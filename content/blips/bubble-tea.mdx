**Bubble Tea** from Charm is our framework for building TUIs in Go. It's based on The Elm Architecture (a model, an update function, and a view), which keeps even complex interfaces predictable and testable. Charm's companion libraries cover the rest of what a terminal application needs: Bubbles for common components, Lip Gloss for styling, and Wish for serving applications over SSH.
