**ITIL 4** is a framework of service management practices organized around a service value system. We're trialing a lightweight subset (incident management, problem management, change enablement, and service level management) to give our operations a common vocabulary and clear handoffs as the infrastructure we run grows. We're adopting the practices, not the bureaucracy: processes stay small, automated where possible, and owned by the teams that run the services.
